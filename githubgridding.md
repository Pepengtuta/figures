|:-:|   → center align  (use this for images)
|:--|   → left align
|--:|   → right align

width="300"  → web screenshots (landscape)
width="150"  → mobile screenshots (portrait)
width="200"  → gifs / mixed

Always end the last cell with  |
Always have a space between "  and  width=
